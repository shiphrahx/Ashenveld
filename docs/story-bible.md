## The six relationships

| Relationship | What you were to each other | What leaving did | What the mechanism shows you |
|---|---|---|---|
| Childhood friend | You ran the east field fence together every summer. They taught you the three-flat-one-angled fire ring. | You said you would write. You wrote twice. | They went to the docks every market day for a year in case you were on a barge. |
| Former lover | The last two years before you left. | You left without saying it was ending, so it never did. | They were at the docks the morning you went. They stood in the warehouse door and did not call out. |
| Sibling | Two years younger. You shared the house on the mill lane. | You left them with the house and your mother's last illness. | Your mother asked for you at the end. They told her you were on your way. |
| Mentor | They taught you your trade, and the fire ring. | They told you to go. You thought they were tired of you. | They sent you away because they already knew what was under the forest. |
| Parent | They raised you in the house on the mill lane. | You left after an argument neither of you finished. | They watched the barge until it was out of sight. They were proud. They have been furious for eleven years. |
| The one who got away | Nothing was ever said. | You left before you had to say it. | They knew. They were waiting for you to say it. |

## Decisions the script rests on

- **D1, why you left.** Crestfall was making you quiet. Nothing happened; you could feel yourself wanting less each year, and one spring you got on a barge before it finished. You told nobody, because there was no event to point at. It is what the curse now does to everyone. Class passages may add a second reason. They must not contradict this one.
- **D2, Maret's arrangement.** Each new moon Maret walks to the tree line alone and gives the forest one memory that matters to her. It takes it; she cannot recall it afterwards. In return the dimming stays shallow inside the camp and the garden grows. She keeps a written list so she knows what she no longer has. The first entry is "my daughter's voice". There are thirty-eight. After the seal she stops paying, and nothing comes back.
- **D3, what [missing person] wanted.** They did not want you to come. The letter left with Maret says so. The cloth at the campsite says "Go home." They also could not finish the ritual alone, and in the end they told the Watcher your name.
- **D4, what the mechanism shows.** One fact per relationship, in the table above.
- **D5, the Watcher** is Joren of Harrow, the man in the old man's diary who "went east and did not come back". He carved the cellar walls and the bone figures.
- **D6, what Sen saw.** A covered boat, three weeks before Day 1, putting one passenger ashore above the docks at night: Seriva.

## People

**Aldous** keeps the Tallow. About sixty, careful, tired, kind in ways he won't admit: he charges three coins under the posted rate and says nothing about it. Talks about weather, prices and wood when he means people. Never asks a direct question.

**Sen** works the docks and has done all his life. Greying, frightened of something he won't name, always looking at the water. He saw Seriva come ashore early.

**Maret** leads the Rootless. In her thirties, weathered, guarded, practical. Came to Crestfall three years ago and takes in the people the town turns away. She pointed [missing person] east and has regretted it for fourteen months. Pays the forest one memory a moon.

**Branch** is Maret's second. A big, unhurried former soldier who came out of the eastern villages with her. States times and numbers exactly. Does not soften anything. Loyal to Maret before anyone.

**Lira** works rope at the camp's edge. In her twenties, partly afflicted, better some days than others. Halting, concrete, says thank you. She drew the Rootless map.

**Edric** is the Covenant priest at the old alderman's hall. In his fifties, composed, precise about probability, never raises his voice. Believes in the purification and has never heard the count for Crestfall.

**Cael** is the Compact lieutenant at the gate. Young, too upright, smiles with the lower half of his face. Says "we", not "I". Knew Vorne was coming.

**Vorne** commands the Compact garrison that arrives on Day 4. In his fifties, sleepless, genuinely trying to save the region; his methods are the problem. Orders phrased as preferences, one-word questions. His daughter is Issa.

**Issa** is seventeen, Vorne's daughter, at the camp with grey at her fingertips. Sharper than her father knows, clear about him, uses contractions.

**Seriva** is a senior Covenant scholar from the capital, in her sixties. She broke the seal twenty years ago trying to strengthen it, and has spent twenty years on a purification that would kill the deeply afflicted. Talks in costs and thresholds, no contractions, until she breaks.

**Pol** is the camp's healer, seventy, formerly the Covenant's archivist. Dry, counts things, compares people to plants. Holds the knowledge of the real ritual and brings the fragment.

**Joren, the Watcher**, sits against the right-hand stone of the ruins gate in Harrow wool. He walked east from Harrow and stayed. He carved the figures and holds one if you give it to him.

**Corvin** is eighty and has kept the preserved-food stall forever. Says exactly what he means in as few words as possible.

**Hanna** sells grain in the market and quietly feeds the camp.

**Wren** was the man everyone liked. He stopped talking two months ago and sits by the Tallow's fire; after Vorne arrives he walks east.

**[missing person]** came back to Ashenveld fourteen months ago and has been in the ruins since, learning the chamber text. Careful at first, plainer as Act 2 goes on. Says "we" about the ritual.

## Voices in brief

Aldous, Sen, Hanna, Lira, Issa and the soldiers use contractions. Maret, Branch, Edric and Seriva mostly don't. Dry humour, rarely, from Aldous, Branch and Pol. Nobody explains their feelings.
